### Who We Are

The Yasma Screen is a Florida-based company specializing in high-quality pool cages, pergolas, and screening services. With years of hands-on experience and a commitment to craftsmanship, we help homeowners protect and upgrade their outdoor spaces with durable, long-lasting materials.

From repairing a damaged screen to building a brand-new pergola or replacing an entire pool enclosure, we bring honesty, precision, and professional service to every project. We treat each home as if it were our own, because the work we do becomes part of how you live and relax outdoors.

#### Florida Born and Florida Focused

Florida's climate is unlike anywhere else. Intense sun, heavy humidity, salt air near the coast, and a serious storm season all take a toll on outdoor structures. Being Florida-based means we understand these conditions firsthand and build for them. We choose materials and methods that hold up to the environment instead of fighting against it.

#### Experience You Can Lean On

Years of hands-on work across countless outdoor projects have taught us what lasts and what fails. That experience shows up in the details, from how a frame is anchored to how a screen is tensioned, and it is what separates a job that looks good on day one from one that still looks good years later.

### What We Do

We focus on the outdoor structures that matter most to Florida homeowners. Our core services are built around protecting your space, expanding how you use it, and keeping it looking its best.

#### Pool Cages

We design, build, repair, and replace aluminum pool cages that keep bugs and debris out while standing up to sun and storms. From a simple rescreen to a full custom enclosure, our goal is a structure that feels open and inviting while delivering real durability.

#### Pergolas

A well built pergola transforms a backyard or patio into a true outdoor living area. We build pergolas that add shade, structure, and style, designed to complement your home and handle Florida weather year after year.

#### Screening Services

Screening is at the heart of what we do. We handle patio and lanai screening, entryway screen doors, rescreening, and screen repairs. The right screen keeps your space comfortable and protected while preserving the views and airflow that make outdoor living enjoyable.

### What We Stand For
