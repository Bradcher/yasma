## Custom Pool Cages

Pool cages are where The Yasma Screen started, and they remain the backbone of our business. We design, engineer, and install custom aluminum pool cage enclosures for homes of every size across Southwest Florida.

A pool cage does more than keep bugs out. It reduces debris in the water, cuts UV exposure on the pool deck, provides a safety barrier for children and pets, and protects the entire pool area from wind-driven rain. In a state where most homes have a pool, a quality cage is not optional. It's essential infrastructure.

Our pool cage services cover the full lifecycle of an enclosure.

### New Custom Pool Cages

Every new cage we build starts with a site visit and detailed measurement. We design the enclosure around your specific pool shape, deck layout, and home architecture. Bay spacing, roof style, color, picture window placement, and door locations are all customized rather than pulled from a generic template. Every cage is engineered to meet local wind load codes, uses powder-coated aluminum framing, and is fastened with 300-series stainless steel hardware throughout.

### Full Cage Replacement

If your existing cage is beyond repair, whether from storm damage, age, or corrosion, we handle full teardown and replacement. This includes removing the old structure, inspecting and repairing the mounting points on your home and deck, and building a brand new cage from the ground up. Many homeowners use a replacement as an opportunity to upgrade to a larger footprint, add picture windows, or change the roof style.

### Rescreening and Repair

Not every cage needs full replacement. Screen mesh degrades over time from UV exposure, and individual panels can tear from debris or weather. We rescreen individual panels or entire cages using premium mesh materials including standard fiberglass, no-see-um mesh, pet-resistant screen, and solar mesh. We also repair damaged frame members, replace worn spline, fix doors that don't close properly, and address hardware corrosion.

### Hurricane Reinforcement

For homeowners who want extra peace of mind heading into storm season, we offer structural reinforcement upgrades. These include cross-bracing at high-stress corners, heavier-gauge uprights in exposed locations, reinforced screen panels with smaller individual sections to limit damage spread, and super gutter integration for better water management during heavy rain.

## Patio Enclosures and Lanais

Not every outdoor space has a pool, but almost every Florida home has a patio or lanai that benefits from screening. We enclose patios, lanais, garden areas, and entryways to create comfortable outdoor living spaces that are protected from insects, debris, and weather.

### Full Patio and Lanai Screening

A screened lanai is one of the most used spaces in a Florida home. We build new enclosures from scratch or replace aging structures with modern aluminum framing and fresh screen. Whether your lanai is a small side porch or a full-width outdoor room, we design the enclosure to match your home's style and your lifestyle.

### Entryway Screen Doors

A quality screen door at your front or side entrance keeps bugs out while letting fresh air flow through the house. We install custom-fit screen doors that match the framing and color of your existing entryway for a clean, integrated look.

### Roll-Down Hurricane Screens

For lanais and patios that need storm protection without permanent shutters, roll-down hurricane screens offer a deployable solution. They mount above the opening and roll down when a storm approaches, providing rated wind protection. When the weather clears, they retract completely out of sight.

### Screen Repairs and Replacement

Torn screens, broken spline, bent frames, and doors that won't latch are all common issues we fix regularly. A quick repair visit is often all it takes to restore a patio enclosure to full function.

### Garden and Gazebo Enclosures

If you have a freestanding gazebo, garden structure, or outdoor room that could benefit from screening, we design and build custom enclosures that fit the existing structure. This is a popular option for homeowners who want a screened reading nook, meditation space, or shaded outdoor dining area.

## Paver Installation

A beautiful pool cage or lanai deserves an equally beautiful surface underneath. We design and install paver surfaces for pool decks, driveways, walkways, patios, and lanais using premium materials built to handle Florida's heat, rain, and humidity.

### Pool Deck Pavers

Travertine, brick, and concrete pavers all offer excellent options for pool decks. Travertine stays cool underfoot and offers natural slip resistance. Brick pavers add a classic look. Concrete pavers provide the widest range of colors, shapes, and patterns at a range of price points. We help homeowners choose the right material for their pool area and install it with proper base preparation, drainage, and sealed joints.

### Driveway and Walkway Pavers

A paver driveway or walkway dramatically upgrades your home's curb appeal and adds real value to the property. We install interlocking paver systems that handle vehicle traffic, resist shifting and settling, and look clean for years. Popular patterns include herringbone, running bond, and basketweave.

### Patio and Lanai Paver Surfaces

Indoor-outdoor flow matters in Florida, and a paver patio that complements your interior flooring creates a seamless transition from inside to outside. We install paver surfaces on existing concrete slabs or new base preparations depending on the project.

### Custom Designs and Layouts

Beyond standard patterns, we create custom paver layouts with borders, inlays, mixed materials, and curved edges that match the architecture of the home and the landscaping around it.

## Pergolas

Aluminum pergolas have become one of the fastest-growing segments of our business. Homeowners across Southwest Florida are adding pergolas over outdoor kitchens, dining areas, fire pits, seating areas, and pool decks to create defined outdoor rooms with shade and architectural interest.

### Custom Aluminum Pergolas

We design and build freestanding and attached pergolas using powder-coated aluminum that won't rot, warp, or attract termites like wood. Our pergola designs range from clean modern flat-bar profiles to more traditional styles, all engineered to meet Florida wind load codes.

### Shade Solutions

Open-beam pergolas filter sunlight naturally, but for homeowners who want more control, we offer louvered roof systems that open and close with a remote, as well as retractable canopy options and motorized sunscreen integration. The goal is always adjustable shade that works with Florida's changing conditions throughout the day.

### Modern Backyard Upgrades

A pergola instantly defines a space and gives it purpose. Adding integrated LED lighting, ceiling fans, speaker mounts, and cable management transforms a basic patio into a true outdoor living room. We handle the structural and electrical planning so everything is built in from day one rather than tacked on later.

### Seating-Ready Designs

Many of our pergola clients want a structure that accommodates deep seating furniture, rocking chairs, swings, or bar-height seating. We design the column spacing, beam height, and overall footprint to match the furniture layout our clients have in mind.

## Gutters for Enclosures

Water management is one of the most overlooked aspects of outdoor structures in Florida. Without proper gutters, rainwater cascading off a pool cage roof or pergola pours directly onto the deck, into the pool, or against the foundation of your home. Over time, that uncontrolled water causes erosion, staining, and structural damage.

### Aluminum and Seamless Gutters

We install seamless aluminum gutters custom-cut on site to fit the exact dimensions of your enclosure. Seamless gutters eliminate the joints where conventional gutters leak, providing cleaner water flow and a much more polished appearance.

### Water Drainage for Pool Cages and Lanais

For pool cage enclosures, we integrate gutter systems directly into the cage roof structure using super gutters that double as structural beams. This approach manages water while adding rigidity to the entire cage. For lanais and patios, we install traditional gutter runs with downspouts directed away from the foundation.

### Gutter Installation and Maintenance

Beyond new installations, we service and maintain existing gutter systems. Florida's tropical vegetation drops leaves, pollen, and debris into gutters constantly, and clogged gutters lead to overflow, fascia damage, and mosquito breeding. Regular cleaning and inspection keeps everything flowing.
