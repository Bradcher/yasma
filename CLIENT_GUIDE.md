# The Yasma Screen — Blog Guide

## Logging In

1. Go to **theyasmascreen.com/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub**
3. If prompted, click **Authorize** — you only see this the first time
4. You're in! Click **Blog Posts** in the left sidebar to see your posts

---

## Writing a New Post

Click **New Blog Post** (top right). Fill in each field:

**Title** *(required)*
The headline of your post. Keep it clear and descriptive — this becomes the page URL too.

**Publish Date** *(required)*
- Set to **today** to publish immediately after saving
- Set to a **future date** to schedule it — it goes live automatically that morning around 7–8 AM

**Featured Image** *(required)*
The main photo for the post. Click the field, then click **Choose an image** to upload one from your computer. See image tips at the bottom of this guide.

**Excerpt** *(required)*
1–2 sentences summarizing the post. This appears on the blog listing page and in Google search results.

**Meta Title** *(optional)*
A custom title for Google. Leave blank and the post title is used automatically.

**Meta Description** *(optional)*
A custom description for Google — aim for 150–160 characters. Leave blank and the excerpt is used automatically.

**Categories** *(optional)*
Pick one or more from the list (Pool Cages, Pergolas, Lanais, Screens, Maintenance, Tips, News).

**Tags** *(optional)*
Freeform keywords — type one and press **Enter** to add it (e.g. `rescreening`, `cape coral`, `hurricane screen`). Add as many as you like.

**Draft** *(toggle)*
- **ON (blue)** = post is hidden from the site — nobody can see it
- **OFF (gray)** = post is live on the site

New posts start as Draft ON by default. When you're ready to publish, flip it off.

**Body** *(required)*
Write your post here. Use the toolbar to add **bold**, *italic*, headings, bullet lists, and links. Press Enter for a new paragraph.

When you're done, click **Save** (top right). The site rebuilds in about 1–2 minutes and your post appears.

---

## Adding Images Inside a Post

1. In the Body editor, click the **image icon** in the toolbar (looks like a mountain)
2. Click **Choose an image** and upload a photo from your computer
3. The image appears in your post — you can add a caption below it if you like

**Tip:** Place images between paragraphs, not in the middle of a sentence.

---

## Categories and Tags

- **Categories** are the broad topics you pick from a list (Pool Cages, Maintenance, etc.). Choose 1–2 per post.
- **Tags** are specific keywords you type yourself (rescreening, cape coral, hurricane prep). Use as many as make sense.

Both create their own archive pages automatically — readers can click a category or tag to see all related posts.

---

## How Scheduling Works

1. Write your post as normal
2. Set the **Publish Date** to a future date (e.g. next Tuesday)
3. Turn **Draft OFF**
4. Click **Save**

The post won't appear on the site yet. Every morning around 7–8 AM, the site automatically rebuilds. On the morning your date arrives, the post goes live — no action needed from you.

---

## Draft vs. Published

| Draft ON | Draft OFF |
|---|---|
| Post is saved but invisible | Post is live on the site |
| Safe to work on it | Appears in blog listing and search |
| You can take your time | Goes live on its publish date |

---

## Editing an Existing Post

1. Log in and click **Blog Posts**
2. Find your post and click it
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Save**

The site rebuilds and shows your changes in 1–2 minutes.

## Deleting a Post

1. Open the post
2. Click the **three dots (⋯)** menu near the top right
3. Click **Delete**

The post is removed from the site on the next rebuild.

---

## Image Guidelines

- **Format:** JPG or WebP preferred (PNG is fine too)
- **Featured image size:** At least 1200px wide, roughly landscape orientation
- **Inline images:** At least 800px wide
- **File size:** Under 3 MB per image — compress at [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) if needed
- **File names:** Use simple names with no spaces (e.g. `pool-cage-before.jpg` not `IMG 4892 copy (2).jpg`)

---

## What To Do If Stuck

- **Post not showing up?** Check that Draft is turned OFF and the Publish Date is today or in the past. Wait 2 minutes for the site to rebuild.
- **Can't log in?** Try a private/incognito window and log in again at theyasmascreen.com/admin
- **Accidentally deleted something?** Let your web developer know — posts can be recovered from the git history
- **Anything else:** Contact Brad at bradleycher@gmail.com
